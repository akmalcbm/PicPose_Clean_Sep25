Privacy Policy

App: PicPose

Effective Date: February 15, 2026

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PicPose collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android application and backend services.

1. Data We Collect

Based on the current app and backend behavior, PicPose may collect and process:

  • Account Data: name/display name, email address, login provider information, profile metadata.
  • Profile Content: profile image uploads and profile fields (for example bio/account type) stored on server infrastructure.
  • Push Notification Data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token, optional linked user ID, and token-sync metadata (platform, app version, device model, OS version, language, country, timezone).
  • Support Data: support form details such as name, email, phone (optional), and message content.
  • Technical/Device Signals: app/network request metadata required for delivery, security, abuse prevention, and service reliability.
  • Ads-Related Signals: ad identifiers/device signals processed by integrated ad SDK partners (for example AdMob/Meta Audience Network), subject to their platform policies.

2. Important Clarification About Images

PicPose can upload and store user profile images on backend servers when users choose to update their profile. Therefore, it is not accurate to claim that all photos always remain only on device.

3. Why We Process Data

  • Account registration, login, and profile management
  • Profile image hosting and profile updates
  • Push notification delivery and token maintenance
  • User support and issue resolution
  • Ads delivery/measurement and operational controls
  • Platform security, fraud prevention, and compliance obligations

4. Legal Bases (GDPR/UK Where Applicable)

  • Contract/Service Delivery: account and core app functionality
  • Legitimate Interests: security, reliability, abuse prevention
  • Consent: where legally required (including ads personalization in regulated regions)
  • Legal Obligation: compliance and lawful requests

5. Ads Consent, CMP, and Regional Privacy Choices

Accepting Terms/Privacy during login is not the same as advertising consent. Advertising consent is handled separately through a Consent Management Platform (CMP), such as Google UMP or an equivalent CMP flow where configured.

For EEA/UK and applicable US state law regions, users can manage or withdraw consent choices through available in-app controls and supported platform privacy settings.

6. Data Sharing

PicPose does not sell personal data. Data may be shared with service providers/partners only as needed to provide app functionality, authentication, notifications, ads operations, support tooling, and legal compliance.

7. Data Retention

  • Account/profile data: retained while account is active, then deleted/anonymized according to deletion workflow.
  • Profile images: retained while linked profile is active, then removed subject to backup retention windows.
  • Push token records: retained while active/needed for notification delivery and safety operations.
  • Support submissions: retained for support, auditing, and compliance needs.
  • Security/log/backup records: retained for limited periods (commonly up to 90 days) before purge/rotation, subject to law.

8. Security

  • HTTPS/TLS used for data in transit
  • Access controls on backend/admin systems
  • No intentional plaintext password storage for account-password auth flows
  • Operational monitoring and hardening practices

No internet transmission/storage method is absolutely secure, but we continuously improve controls.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction/objection, portability (where applicable), and withdrawal of consent-based processing.

10. Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion via in-app options (where available) or by contacting support at picposeapp@gmail.com. We aim to process verified deletion requests within 7 business days, subject to legal/security retention exceptions.

11. Children and Age Policy

PicPose is intended for a Teen+ audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
